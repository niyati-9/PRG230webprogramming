Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some common questions about Mechanic Sathi.

❓

Is Mechanic Sathi free to use?

Yes, the app is free to download and use. However, service charges at workshops may apply.

📡

Does it work offline?

Yes! Mechanic Sathi uses offline maps and local storage to function without internet connectivity.

🔧

Are workshops verified?

All listed workshops go through a strict verification process to ensure quality and trustworthiness.

📍

Can I find a mechanic in remote areas?

Absolutely. We’ve partnered with workshops across major highways and rural regions in Nepal.

🆘

What if I need urgent help?

Use the emergency call feature or in-app live chat to get connected to nearby assistance instantly.

📱

How do I download the app?

Visit the Download page to get the Android or iOS version of Mechanic Sathi.
