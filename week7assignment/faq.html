Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some common questions about Mechanic Sathi and how it works.

Quick Answers

Most commonly asked questions at a glance

Is Mechanic Sathi free to use?

Yes, the app is completely free to download and use. Service charges apply only when you avail help from workshops.

Does it work offline?

Yes! Mechanic Sathi uses offline maps and local storage to function without internet connectivity during emergencies.

Are mechanics verified?

All listed mechanics go through a strict verification process including background checks and skill assessments.

How accurate is location tracking?

Our GPS system is accurate within 3-5 meters and works across all 75+ districts of Nepal, even in remote areas.

How long does help take to arrive?

Average response time is 15 minutes in urban areas and 30-45 minutes in remote locations across Nepal.

Is my data safe?

Yes, all personal data is encrypted using military-grade security and we never share your information with third parties.

Detailed FAQ

Comprehensive answers to help you understand Mechanic Sathi better

General Questions

Mechanic Sathi is Nepal's first comprehensive roadside assistance app. It connects vehicle owners with verified mechanics and workshops across the country. Simply open the app, describe your problem, and we'll connect you with the nearest qualified mechanic. The app works both online and offline, making it reliable even in remote areas with poor network coverage.

Mechanic Sathi covers all 75+ districts of Nepal, from urban centers like Kathmandu and Pokhara to remote mountain regions. Our network includes over 2,000 verified mechanics and workshops across highways, rural roads, and tourist routes. We're constantly expanding our coverage to reach even the most remote areas.

Mechanic Sathi supports all types of vehicles commonly found in Nepal including motorcycles, scooters, cars, jeeps, buses, trucks, and even heavy machinery. Our mechanics are trained to handle various makes and models, both local and international brands.

App Usage

Requesting assistance is simple: 1) Open the Mechanic Sathi app, 2) Allow location access, 3) Select your vehicle type, 4) Describe your problem from the list or type a custom description, 5) View nearby mechanics with ratings and estimated arrival times, 6) Select a mechanic and confirm your request. You'll receive real-time updates on the mechanic's location and arrival time.

Yes! Mechanic Sathi is designed to work offline. The app stores essential data locally, including mechanic locations and contact information for your current area. While some advanced features require internet connectivity, core functions like finding nearby mechanics and making emergency calls work entirely offline.

Once you confirm a mechanic, you'll see their real-time location on the map screen. The app provides estimated arrival time, allows direct calling, and sends push notifications with status updates. You can also message the mechanic through the app's secure messaging system without revealing your personal phone number.

Yes, Mechanic Sathi is available in both English and Nepali languages. You can switch between languages in the app settings. We're also working on adding support for other regional languages to better serve Nepal's diverse linguistic communities.

Payment & Pricing

The Mechanic Sathi app is completely free to download and use. You only pay for the actual mechanical services provided by the workshop or mechanic. Pricing is transparent and shown upfront before you confirm the service. Emergency callout fees typically range from NPR 200-500 depending on location and time.

You can pay mechanics directly in cash, which is the most common method. We also support digital payments through popular Nepali platforms like eSewa, Khalti, and mobile banking. Some mechanics accept credit cards, and this information is displayed in their profile.

No, there are no hidden charges. All costs are clearly displayed before you confirm a service request. The only charges you pay are directly to the mechanic for their services and parts. Mechanic Sathi doesn't add any commission or booking fees to your bill.

Safety & Security

Our verification process includes: 1) Identity verification with government-issued documents, 2) Skill assessment tests, 3) Background checks, 4) Workshop inspection for registered businesses, 5) Customer feedback monitoring, and 6) Regular re-verification. Only mechanics who pass all criteria are allowed on the platform.

If you're unsatisfied, you can: 1) Rate and review the mechanic in the app, 2) Report issues through our 24/7 support system, 3) Request a refund if the service was substandard, 4) Get connected with another mechanic for a second opinion. We take all complaints seriously and may remove mechanics who consistently receive poor reviews.

Safety features include: 1) One-tap SOS button that alerts emergency services and your emergency contacts, 2) Real-time location sharing with trusted contacts, 3) In-app panic button, 4) 24/7 customer support hotline, 5) Integration with local police and traffic police networks, and 6) Anonymous reporting system for any suspicious activity.
