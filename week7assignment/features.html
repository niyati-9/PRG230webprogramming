GPS-Based Workshop Discovery

Locate the nearest verified mechanical workshops based on your GPS coordinates, even in remote areas of Nepal.

Mechanic Sathi works without internet connection, making it reliable during emergencies in network-dead zones.

Free to Use

The app is completely free to download and use. Service charges apply only when availing help from local workshops.

All mechanics and workshops are verified through our rigorous screening process to ensure quality service.
